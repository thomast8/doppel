# Doppel 1.1.5

- Makes a coloured shortcut assigned to the built-in Browser bring its existing official ChatGPT window forward instead of appearing to do nothing.
- Verifies the exact running ChatGPT process before focusing it and shows a clear warning if its identity has changed or macOS refuses the request.
- Keeps the white ChatGPT icon as the running-process indicator; the coloured shortcut remains a focus shortcut without a running dot.
